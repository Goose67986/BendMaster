# BendPro 360 Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This policy applies to BendPro 360 — Press Brake Calculator, Android package `com.bendmasterpro.app`.

## Information handled by the app

Version 1.0 does not collect or send personal information to the developer. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, location tracking, or in-app purchases. Calculations run on your device. Custom material names, K-factors and tensile-strength values are saved in the app's local storage.

## Device backup

Android's backup feature is enabled for this version. Depending on your device and Google account settings, Android may back up app data through your device's backup service. The developer does not receive or control that backup. You can manage backup through Android settings.

## Sharing and retention

The app does not share local calculation or material data with the developer or third parties. Local material records remain until you remove them, clear the app's data or uninstall it. Backup copies, if any, are subject to your backup service's settings.

## Support

If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to include are used to answer your request. Do not send sensitive personal information unnecessarily. To request deletion of a support correspondence record, contact nngbusiness@gmail.com.

## Children and changes

The app is intended as a fabrication tool and is not designed to gather information from children. If future releases change the way data is handled, this policy will be updated.

## Contact

For privacy questions: nngbusiness@gmail.com
