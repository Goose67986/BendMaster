# Privacy Policy

## BendPro 360 — Press Brake Calculator

**Effective date: October 14, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how BendPro 360 — Press Brake Calculator ("BendPro 360," "the app," "we," or "us") handles information. It applies to the current Android app, version 1.0.2, with package ID `com.Bendpro360.myapp`.

The developer can be contacted at **nngbusiness@gmail.com**.

## 1. Information processed by the app

BendPro 360 is an offline fabrication calculator. You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address, or other personal information to use it.

The app processes values you enter, such as material thickness, material type, tooling dimensions, bend angles, bend lengths, flange lengths, K-factors, and tensile strength, to calculate and display results on your device. These values and results are not transmitted to the developer.

Custom material records, including material names, categories, K-factors, and tensile-strength values, are saved in the app's local storage so they remain available when you reopen the app. Other calculation inputs are held in memory during use.

The current app does not collect personal information for the developer, send usage analytics or crash reports to the developer, track your location, access your contacts, camera, or microphone, or collect advertising identifiers.

## 2. How information is used

Information entered in the app is used to perform calculations, show diagrams and results, and maintain your custom material library on your device. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

The current version contains no advertising, analytics services, account system, or in-app purchases.

## 3. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell or rent personal information. The app does not upload your calculation inputs, results, or custom material records to our servers or share them through advertising or analytics services.

Google Play and Android may independently process information when you download, install, update, or use their services. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies, including [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

If you visit this policy on GitHub or GitHub Pages, GitHub may process standard website-access information under [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). That website processing is separate from the app's offline calculations.

## 4. Android device backup

Android backup is enabled for this app. Depending on your device, operating system, and backup settings, Android may copy locally stored app data to your device's backup service and restore it when you reinstall the app or change devices.

The developer does not receive or have access to these backup copies. Backup storage, retention, protection, and deletion are governed by your backup provider and device settings. You can review and manage backup options in your device settings.

## 5. Retention and deletion

Custom material records remain in local app storage until you remove them in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Clearing app storage resets your local material library. Backup copies may remain separately and may be restored later; manage those copies through your backup provider.

Calculation inputs held only in memory are not retained as a saved calculation history.

Because the app has no user accounts and the developer receives no calculation or material data, there is no developer-held account or calculation database to delete.

## 6. Support correspondence

If you email **nngbusiness@gmail.com**, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your request, troubleshoot reported problems, and maintain necessary support records.

Support correspondence is processed through our email service provider and retained for as long as needed to handle the request and related follow-up, or to meet applicable legal requirements. It is not used for advertising or sold. You can contact the same address to request access to or deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention requirements. Please avoid including unnecessary sensitive information.

## 7. Security

The app uses Android's app-private storage for saved material records. Keeping your device protected with current software and a screen lock helps protect this information. No device, storage system, or email service can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 8. Children's privacy

BendPro 360 is a fabrication tool and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect children's personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support correspondence, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features or information-handling practices change. Updated policies will be posted at this location with a revised effective date. Any future advertising, account, purchase, or data-collection features will be described in the applicable updated policy before those practices begin, with any notices or consent required by law.
