SHORT DESCRIPTION: Press brake bend deduction, flat length, tonnage and material tools.

FULL DESCRIPTION:
BendPro 360 is an offline press brake calculator for sheet metal operators, fabricators and programmers.

Calculate bend allowance, bend deduction, estimated forming tonnage, effective inside radius and minimum flange length. Use the flat pattern calculator to work from outside flange dimensions and view formed-part and flat-layout diagrams.

Features:
• Bend allowance and bend deduction calculations
• Flat length calculations for multiple flanges
• Estimated required tonnage and a 15% allowance
• Suggested V-die openings based on thickness and material family
• Warnings when the selected die opening is below the app's minimum guideline
• Editable material K-factors and tensile strength
• Add and remove materials stored locally on your device
• Dark interface with blue and orange accents
• Offline operation without an account

Version 1.0 uses inch-based dimension inputs. Tooling suggestions and calculated values are estimates. Confirm material properties, actual bend results, and machine/tooling limits before production use.

No advertising, subscriptions or paid upgrades in version 1.0.
